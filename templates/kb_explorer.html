Mathematical Formalism in CareerWise AI
1. MYCIN Certainty Factor Combination Formula

Certainty factors represent inexact reasoning and confidence under partial information. When multiple independent rules $R_1$ and $R_2$ conclude hypothesis $H$:

CF_combined(H) = CF(H, R1) + CF(H, R2) × [1 − CF(H, R1)]

This guarantees asymptotic convergence toward 1.0 (absolute certainty) without ever exceeding the ceiling.

2. Composite Suitability Score (Multi-Criteria MCDA)

The composite score harmonizes rule inference with continuous competency benchmarks and university coursework:

Score = 0.40 × CF% + 0.35 × SkillMatch% + 0.15 × Academics% + 0.10 × Interest%

Critical skills carry a 1.5× multiplier to prevent students with major blockers from ranking highly.
